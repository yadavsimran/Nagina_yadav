Representation of Nepal in:

01

Germany

International Study Tour on Federalism & Decentralisation

Joined a delegation of South Asian parliamentarians for an international study tour organized by the Friedrich Naumann Foundation (FNF), focused on federalism, decentralization, and strengthening subnational and local governance. Engaged with German parliamentarians and institutions to exchange perspectives on federal governance, local empowerment, and effective cooperation across different levels of government.

02

United Arab Emirates

Arab-Asia Parliamentarians Meeting

Represented Nepal as a parliamentarian at the Arab-Asia Parliamentarians Meeting, contributing to regional discussions on sexual and reproductive health and climate change resilience. Engaged with parliamentarians from across Asia and the Arab region on legislative approaches to addressing population and reproductive health challenges, strengthening climate resilience, and protecting vulnerable communities.

03

Pakistan

Bilateral Exchange on Gender-Responsive Budgeting

Engaged in a bilateral parliamentary exchange between Nepal and Pakistan focused on gender-responsive budgeting, inclusive fiscal governance, and women’s political participation. Contributed to discussions on Nepal’s experience with gender-responsive budgeting while exploring shared challenges and opportunities for strengthening gender equality through parliamentary and policy processes.
